Welcome to PAJI

The Punjabi Association of Jacksonville Indians is a non-profit organization dedicated to preserving and sharing the rich cultural traditions of Punjab. We host festivals, workshops, and charitable initiatives that bring our community together throughout the year.

Whether you were born in Punjab, have family roots in the region, or simply love the culture — you are always welcome here. Our doors are open to all who wish to celebrate, learn, and grow together.

What We Do

Cultural Festivals

From Vaisakhi and Diwali to Lohri and Gurpurab, our festivals draw hundreds of families for authentic food, music, and dance every year.

Image

Youth Programs

Engaging the next generation through Punjabi language classes, Bhangra workshops, and youth leadership initiatives for children of all ages.

Community Outreach

Partnering with local organizations for food drives, scholarship programs, free health screenings, and civic engagement across Jacksonville.
