Generosity Keeps Our Heritage Alive.

At the Punjabi Association of Jacksonville (PAJI), every festival, youth initiative, and community outreach program is powered by your generosity. Your contribution keeps our rich culture thriving in North Florida and helps us serve those in need through selfless service (Seva).

We are deeply grateful for your continuous love, generosity, and commitment to building a stronger, more connected Punjabi community in Jacksonville.
